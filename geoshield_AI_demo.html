Model Principle

A live disaster feed alone cannot describe total risk. GeoShield therefore combines observed events with baseline hazard exposure, vulnerability and coping-capacity indicators.

This is a planning-support model and does not replace official evacuation orders, hydrological forecasts, engineering assessments or field verification.

Interpretation

Red-zone status represents a decision-support priority score. It is not an official evacuation order.

Live events are separated from baseline susceptibility so countries such as Nepal are not incorrectly treated as "safe" simply because a specific flood event is not currently visible.
